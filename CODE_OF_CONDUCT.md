# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Summary

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, never people.
- Assume good faith. Ask before accusing.
- Harassment, discrimination, and personal attacks are not tolerated.
- The maintainers have final say on whether conduct violates this policy.

## Reporting

Email **tathagatmaitray@gmail.com** with a description and any context (links, screenshots). Reports are confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may, at their discretion, warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban contributors whose conduct violates this policy.
